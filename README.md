# Cat-Warehouse-Kanban
(ITEC 3020 Assignment #2 - 2026) An interactive Kanban-style task application using only React and no use of third-party libraries.
---------------------------------
As the heavy blanket of night begins to bleed into the beginnings of dawn, and the static, fluorescent buzz of the monitor begins to loosen 
It's hold on a pliant and sleep-deprived mind, productivity begins its ascent to be known in the morning light. 

With the entrance of Cat_Warehouse comes its counterpart, Cat_Warehouse Kanban, a novel and fresh new play on the Japanese lean method to manage 
and improve work across human systems. With many felines being crepuscular, meaning they thrive in the wee hours of dawn and dusk, it is almost imperative 
that they find themselves dealing with a quick boost of energy whilst trying to savour the last morsels of night. 

Though Cat_Warehouse encourages the lone night owls to seize the freedom of the dark and stay up late, teetering on the edges of other fictional worlds, 
Cat_Warehouse Kanban wishes for those who are still awake to see the sun rise, to step back and bask in the light. 

Embrace the ethereal hours of the morning and use that burst of energy to seize productivity by the scruff and rid yourself of any dust that has gathered around 
the edge of your room. The time to play may be over, but the day has just begun.
